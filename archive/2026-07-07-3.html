The field is quietly bifurcating — between those building intuition-first AI systems and those still waiting for formal playbooks to arrive.

🗞️  Week in Context

Two tensions surface this week. First, non-developers are already shipping agentic AI systems through accumulated intuition — no methodology required — while enterprises continue pouring budget into generative AI programs that can't produce measurable outcomes, repeating exactly the mistakes Lean Startup diagnosed fifteen years ago. Second, the UX role itself is under definitional pressure: Shift UX 2026 frames UX as a "shared toolkit," not a job title. For a team mid-transition, the signal is pointed — fluency built through doing is outpacing fluency acquired through waiting, and leadership credibility increasingly belongs to those who can name what's failing and propose something testable.
