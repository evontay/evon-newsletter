This Week's Theme

The exit door is being designed out of every system — and UX teams need to decide whether they're building the lock or the key.

🗞️  Week in Context

Two threads pull in opposite directions this week. The practitioner conversation is tightening around specificity — dogfooding's limits, OKLCH's blind spots, the granular mechanics of what design tools actually can and cannot do. Meanwhile, the bigger frame is about dissolution: AI erasing the seam between online and offline, between tool and author, between user and system. For a team mid-transition, this tension is the work. The risk isn't falling behind on tools — it's losing the analytical distance to see what the tools are doing to the experience, and to the people inside it.
