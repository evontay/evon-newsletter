This Week's Theme

The field is converging on a single uncomfortable truth — AI accelerates execution, but human judgment, cultural awareness, and coherence-making are what separate good design from harmful or forgettable design.

🗞️  Week in Context

This week's signals cluster around a quiet tension: the faster AI makes things buildable, the more consequential the decisions about *what* to build and *how* to frame it. NNG's prototyping piece and Merholz on coherence pull in opposite directions — one says test earlier, the other says someone needs to hold the strategic thread before testing means anything. Underneath both sits the UX Collective's culture piece: AI systems carry encoded defaults that most teams aren't interrogating. The field is collectively asking who owns quality and meaning when velocity is no longer the constraint.
