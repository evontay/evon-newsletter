This Week's Theme

The field is quietly negotiating what designers should own — their outputs, their process, or their thinking — as AI absorbs more of each.

🗞️  Week in Context

Three distinct threads this week converge on a single anxiety: attachment. NN/g's psychological ownership piece warns against gripping the wrong things; the design review article reframes critique as collaborative sense-making rather than territorial defence; and the feature factory piece locates dysfunction not in product decisions but in how organisations distribute (or diffuse) accountability. Meanwhile, AI tools are dissolving familiar boundaries — images, code, decisions. When the artefact is easier to generate, the designer's claim to ownership must shift upstream, toward framing, judgment, and the quality of the question asked.
