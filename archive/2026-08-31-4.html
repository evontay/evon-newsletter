This Week's Theme

Speed and AI capability are outpacing the judgment needed to use them well — and design's survival depends on closing that gap.

🗞️  Week in Context

Three overlapping tensions define this week's signals. First, AI is accelerating output faster than teams can evaluate quality — creating what NN/g calls "UX debt" and what Tara Seshan frames as faster failure. Second, design's value remains structurally illegible to organisations, and AI is sharpening that crisis: when machines can generate interfaces, what exactly is the designer's irreplaceable contribution? Merholz's two-part legibility series lands hard here. Third, a quieter counterpoint: craft, judgment, and human research aren't diminished by AI — they're the thing AI cannot replicate. The field is collectively arguing that fluency without discernment is a liability.
