This Week's Theme

Design's power is real but precarious — the field must choose between being consumed by execution or stepping up to lead.

🗞️  Week in Context

Two contradictory signals collide this week. One camp argues design is quietly ascending — elevated to strategic function as AI reshapes product organisations. The other counters that designers are largely to blame for their own diminishment, too absorbed in AI discourse and too absent from decisions that matter. The Agile trap piece adds a third layer: structural sprint cadences actively prevent designers from doing the reflective work that would earn them strategic credibility. Together, these signals suggest the leverage moment is real — but it will be claimed by designers who stop executing reactively and start shaping systems deliberately.
