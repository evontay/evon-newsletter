This Week's Theme

The field is collectively renegotiating what design expertise *is* — not just what designers *do* — as AI dissolves the boundaries between brief, tool, and outcome.

🗞️  Week in Context

Two fault lines dominate this week's signals. First, a credentials crisis: senior designers are hitting a ceiling not because their skills degraded, but because the scope of the role silently expanded around them — and AI is accelerating that expansion, demanding systems thinking over execution. Second, a communication crisis: UX teams are still reporting activity while stakeholders want business outcomes. Read together, these aren't separate problems. A team that can't articulate its impact *and* can't operate at expanded scope will be doubly marginalised in AI-native organisations. For MosAIc, both gaps are live.
